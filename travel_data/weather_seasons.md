# Global Travel Seasons & Weather Guide

## Cherry Blossom Season (Japan)

**Timing:** Late March to mid-April, progressing from south (Kyushima first bloom ~March 20) to north (Hokkaido ~May 5). Tokyo peak bloom typically March 28-April 5; Kyoto April 1-10.

**Best viewing locations:**
- Tokyo: Ueno Park (800 trees, lit at night), Shinjuku Gyoen (1,100 trees, 12 varieties extended bloom), Meguro River (800 trees lining canal, Nakameguro nighttime illumination)
- Kyoto: Maruyama Park (giant weeping cherry tree, evening food stalls), Philosopher's Path (2km canal-side walk under 500 trees), Kiyomizu-dera temple (panoramic hillside view)
- Osaka: Osaka Castle Park (3,000 trees), Expo 70 Commemorative Park

**Planning tips:** Book hotels 3-4 months ahead (peak season). Hanami (花見 blossom viewing picnics) requires early morning ground claiming at popular parks. Weather unpredictable -- rain can end bloom in 2 days. Carry umbrella.

## Monsoon Seasons

**Southeast Asia (June-October):**
- Thailand: heavy afternoon downpours 1-2 hours, then clearing. Bangkok street flooding common but temporary. September wettest month. Advantage: fewer tourists, lower prices, lush green landscapes.
- Vietnam: north (June-August), central (September-December peak), south (May-November). Central Vietnam gets heaviest rain -- avoid Hue/Hoi An October-November.
- Myanmar: June-September, peak rain August. Bagan temples less crowded but dusty access roads become muddy. Inner Myanmar still has 4-5 sunny hours daily.
- Indonesia: dry season actually June-September (reverse monsoon pattern). Bali best June-September. Avoid December-February (wet season).

**India (July-September):**
- Southwest monsoon hits Kerala June 1, reaches Delhi by late June, covers entire country by mid-July. Mumbai receives 2,200mm rain in 3 months. Rajasthan stays relatively dry. Ladakh/Spiti Valley only accessible June-September (road snow-free). Hill stations (Mussoorie, Shimla) pleasant during monsoon with misty beauty.

**East Asia typhoon season:**
- Japan: August-October peak. Most hit Okinawa/Kyushu first, weaken moving north. Monitor Japan Meteorological Agency alerts. Travel insurance with typhoon coverage essential. Railway suspension common during strong typhoons.

## European Seasons

**Summer Peak (July-August):**
- Mediterranean coast (Spain, Italy, Greece, France): 28-38°C, crowded, expensive. Hotels 50-100% pricier than spring. Beaches packed by 10 AM.
- Scandinavia/Northern Europe: mild (18-25°C), long days (Scandinavian midnight sun in June), perfect for hiking and fjords. Book early -- short peak season means limited accommodation.
- Better alternatives: May-June or September for Mediterranean (warm but not scorching, fewer crowds, lower prices). Called "shoulder season" -- the smart traveler's choice.

**Winter (December-February):**
- Southern Spain/Canary Islands: 18-24°C, sunny, Europe's winter escape. Canary Islands called "eternal spring."
- Alpine ski season: December-March peak. Austrian/French/Swiss Alps best January-February for snow reliability. Italian Dolomites excellent. Book ski lodges by October.
- Northern lights (Aurora Borealis): September-March in Iceland, Norway (Tromsø), Finland (Lapland), Swedish Lapland. September/October equinox periods statistically most active. Need clear skies and darkness.

## Americas Seasons

**Caribbean hurricane season (August-October):**
- Peak hurricane activity September. Aruba/Bonaire/Curaçao (ABC islands) below hurricane belt -- safe year-round. Bermuda: June-November risk window but direct hits rare.
- Travel insurance with hurricane coverage recommended. Many resorts offer "hurricane guarantees" (free return visit if evacuated).

**USA patterns:**
- National Parks: Yellowstone/Grand Teton June-September (snow closes roads otherwise). Yosemite spring waterfall peak April-May. Grand Canyon South Rim open year-round; North Rim closed October-May.
- Florida: pleasant October-April; brutally hot/humid June-September with afternoon thunderstorms. Hurricane risk August-October.
- Hawaii: year-round 24-30°C. Wet season October-March (brief showers); dry season April-September. Kauai north shore wettest island.

**South America:**
- Patagonia: November-March (Southern Hemisphere summer). Trekking season December-February. Strong winds year-round.
- Amazon: wet season December-May (higher water, boat access to flooded forests); dry season June-November (hiking trails open, more wildlife visible at shrinking water sources). Both seasons offer different experiences.
- Machu Picchu: dry season May-September best for hiking; wet season November-March has clouds/rain but fewer visitors. Closed February for maintenance.

## Seasonal Packing Quick Reference

**Tropical (year-round warm, 25-35°C):**
- Quick-dry clothing, UV protection, insect repellent, sandals + one pair closed shoes, rain jacket
- Southeast Asia, Caribbean, South Asia, Central America

**Temperate summer (15-30°C):**
- Light layers, one warm layer for evenings, comfortable walking shoes, sun protection, light rain jacket
- Europe summer, North America summer, Japan spring/autumn

**Cold winter (-5 to 10°C):**
- Thermal base layers, insulated jacket, waterproof boots, gloves, hat, scarf, warm socks
- Northern Europe winter, North America winter, Japan winter, Alpine regions

**Extreme cold (-20 to -5°C):**
- Heavy insulated parka, thermal layers (top + bottom), insulated waterproof boots, balaclava, mittens (warmer than gloves), wool socks, hand/foot warmers
- Arctic travel, deep winter in Nordic/Russian/Canadian regions
